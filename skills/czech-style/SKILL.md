# Professional Czech

Use for customer-facing Czech documentation. Do not use for English skill files,
code, or tooling instructions.

## Choose the register

- `documentation`: precise, concise, explanatory Czech; retain necessary domain
  and technical terms and explain them for the intended reader.
- `offer`: executive, outcome-first, credible marketing tone; remove, translate,
  or move technical vocabulary that does not affect the decision. Never turn
  unsupported benefits or certainty into persuasive copy.

Read [resources/register.md](resources/register.md) for formal vs colloquial
language and [resources/jargon.md](resources/jargon.md) when the audience is not
technical. Preserve terminology genuinely used by the customer's industry.

## Protect grammar and typography

After any Czech term rename, read
[resources/agreement.md](resources/agreement.md) and check every agreeing
adjective, pronoun, participle, and verb; global substitution is not sufficient.

Apply [resources/typography.md](resources/typography.md) to quotations, dashes,
ranges, units, and non-breaking spaces where the output format supports them.

## Final pass

Check headings and table labels first, then body text. One sentence should carry
one main idea and lead with its point. Report material register, terminology,
agreement, or typography changes without claiming a full factual review.
