# Business review criteria

- The opening states the decision/outcome before background.
- Claims, benefits, numbers, and dates have identifiable evidence or are marked
  as assumptions/proposals.
- Current and target state are distinct.
- Value is expressed from the affected stakeholder's perspective.
- Risks and exclusions are visible, not buried.
- Business facts have one owner and dependent sections link to them.
- An offer is understandable to a decision-maker without unexplained technical
  vocabulary and covers the shared commercial responsibilities.
