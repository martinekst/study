# Offer deliverable

Apply this guidance across every enabled section when
`deliverable_type: offer`.

## High-level content contract

The portal must make it easy to find:

1. the decision and executive summary;
2. customer problem and desired outcome;
3. value and credible differentiators;
4. proposed scope or options;
5. price, timing, assumptions, and exclusions where applicable;
6. evidence or a sample output;
7. a clear next step.

These are content responsibilities, not mandatory folder names. Preserve the
existing portal or supplied reference as the lower-level structure. With no
reference, propose the smallest structure that covers the responsibilities.

## Language

Lead with customer outcomes. Prefer plain business language; explain, link, or
remove technical terms that do not affect the decision. Marketing language must
remain evidence-backed: never invent benefits, references, delivery certainty,
or numbers.
