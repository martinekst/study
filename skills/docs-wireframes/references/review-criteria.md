# Wireframe review criteria

- Every visible field, action, state, and permission is supported by an owning
  functional page or explicitly labelled as a proposal.
- The frame represents one named screen/state and links back to that owner.
- Hierarchy and task flow are understandable without decorative fidelity.
- Loading, empty, error, success, disabled, and permission states are included
  when they affect the documented journey.
- SVG output is safe, readable at its target size, and free of clipped or
  overlapping labels.
- A screenshot reference is adapted to documented behavior rather than copied
  as unsupported truth.
