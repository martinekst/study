# Pricing review criteria

- Every priced item traces to scope.
- Units, rates, currency, tax, rounding, contingency, and validity are explicit.
- Totals recompute exactly and variants use the same comparison basis.
- Assumptions, exclusions, dependencies, and material uncertainty are visible.
- Offer summaries reconcile with detailed calculations.
- No blank is silently interpreted as zero and no estimate is presented as a
  commitment without the stated basis.
