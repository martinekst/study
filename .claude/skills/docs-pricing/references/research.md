# Estimation research and uncertainty

Research only questions that materially change effort, architecture, licensing,
or risk. Typical examples are third-party product fit, integration limitations,
platform capabilities, migration tooling, licensing, or compliance constraints.

For every research item record:

- the estimate item and decision it affects;
- question and acceptance criteria for an answer;
- sources with retrieval/version date;
- verified fact, inference, and unresolved uncertainty separately;
- buy/configure/build alternatives and their scope consequences;
- resulting estimate change or a bounded spike needed before commitment.

Prefer vendor documentation, contracts, and tested examples over summaries. A
feature list does not prove fit: check limits, editions, API access, data model,
authentication, export, support, hosting region, and pricing basis relevant to the
use case.

Do not hide a spike inside implementation effort. Define its timebox, question,
output, stopping condition, and what estimate will be revised afterward. If
research cannot resolve the uncertainty, retain a range and state the trigger for
re-estimation.
