# Business page pattern

```markdown
# <Decision-oriented title>

<One paragraph: what the reader should understand or decide.>

## Why it matters

<Observed problem/opportunity and evidence.>

## What changes

<Current → target outcome; label proposals and unknowns.>

## Evidence and measurement

<Sources, baseline/target only when supported, how success will be checked.>

## Assumptions, risks, and open decisions

<Only material items owned by this page.>

## Related documentation

<Links to canonical functional, technical, pricing, or roadmap pages.>
```

Remove empty headings; do not fill them with generic text.
