# Business section structure

Prefer an existing coherent tree. For a new ordinary documentation portal, use
the smallest set of pages that covers:

1. executive summary and decision;
2. current situation and measurable problem;
3. intended future situation;
4. value, outcomes, and measurement approach;
5. stakeholders and affected roles;
6. risks, assumptions, and open decisions;
7. roadmap or next steps when evidence exists.

Merge topics when separate pages would be thin. Split only when navigation or
ownership materially improves. Folder/file prefixes control menu order.

The business section owns business facts. Functional pages link to roles/rules;
technical pages link to constraints; pricing links to scope and assumptions.
